Campus Segregation Architecture

Configured 3-stream mapping policy:

1. Dry Recyclables (Blue)

Classes: cardboard, paper, plastic. High circular recovery potential.

2. Glass & Metals (Green)

Classes: glass, metal. High value smelting and cullet reuse.

3. Residual Waste (Gray)

Classes: trash. Sanitary landfill or municipal disposal.

EcoCoins & Circular Rationale

Incentive ledger policies for campus students:

Plastic & Metals +15 EcoCoins / item
Cardboard, Paper, Glass +10 EcoCoins / item
Residual Trash 0 EcoCoins

*Note: Rewards are credited strictly once per verified, accepted disposal event. Uncertain or rejected items receive 0 coins.
